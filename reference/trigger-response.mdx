LifeSphere's power lies in its ability to turn biometric signals, environmental readings, and security events into coordinated physical and digital responses without requiring the user to press a button or speak a command. This page catalogs every trigger and its downstream action.

## Vital32 triggers

| Trigger | Response | Subsystem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Freefall then impact followed by 3 seconds of near-zero movement | fall_detected flag set; rover fall protocol dispatched | Vital32 publishes; HollowRover responds |
| Anomaly score exceeds 0.7 | Soft alert generated for caregiver review | AI layer |
| Anomaly score exceeds 0.9 | Emergency protocol dispatch | AI layer; HollowRover may respond |
| Seizure probability exceeds 0.85 | Seizure protocol triggered; rover prepares cushioning deployment | Vital32 publishes; HollowRover responds |
| GSR exceeds 0.6 | HollowCore publishes play_calming_audio to SphereAI speaker | Vital32 publishes; HollowCore and SphereAI respond |
| SpO2 drops below 94.0 | Air purifier relay activated through MQTT | Vital32 publishes; HollowCore responds |
| Sleep detected via HRV plus accelerometer immobility | Lights off; main door locked | Vital32 publishes; HollowCore responds |
| Skin temperature below 35.5 degrees Celsius | AC thermostat raised | Vital32 publishes; HollowCore responds |
| Cortisol estimate exceeds 18.0 | Warm dim lighting plus lower AC temperature to calm user | Vital32 publishes; HollowCore responds |

## Why this matters

Understanding triggers and responses helps families, caregivers, and administrators see how LifeSphere converts silent biological and environmental changes into timely physical support. The system is designed to disappear into the background of daily life: the user never issues commands, yet lights dim when stress rises, a rover arrives after a fall, and an air filter starts when oxygen saturation drops.

## How it connects to the rest of LifeSphere

Every trigger in the table above flows through the shared biological state in Firebase. Vital32 writes biometric flags; HollowRover subscribes and acts; SphereAI reads emotional context to modulate its own responses; EntryGuard pushes alert payloads with UWB zone and floor references; HollowCore translates state changes into MQTT relay commands. The two-channel communication model, cloud plus local MQTT, ensures that urgent triggers reach actuators even if internet latency fluctuates.

## Limits

The specific internal steps of many protocols are not documented. For example, exactly what cushioning deployment entails during the seizure protocol, or the exact de-escalation steps in a stress-relief protocol, are not specified in the source. Thresholds for fire and gas alerts are described as existing but no numerical values are given. Human authorization is required before any defensive action beyond the passive barrier, but the exact authorization mechanism is not detailed.
