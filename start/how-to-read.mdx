This page offers three guided paths through the LifeSphere site depending on how much time you have and what you want to understand.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="5 minutes: quick orientation">
    If you only have a few minutes, read these three pages in order:

    1. **Introduction** (/) - What LifeSphere is, the five subsystems, and the zero-interaction principle.
    2. **Why LifeSphere Exists** (/start/why-lifesphere-exists) - The human need behind the system.
    3. **Impact and Who It Serves** (/start/impact) - Who benefits and why it matters.

    This path gives you the essential context without technical depth.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="20 minutes: how it works">
    For a solid understanding of the system's design and mechanics, follow this order:

    1. **Introduction** (/)
    2. **Why LifeSphere Exists** (/start/why-lifesphere-exists)
    3. **The Problem in Bangladesh** (/start/problem-in-bangladesh)
    4. **Why Existing Devices Fall Short** (/start/why-existing-devices-fall-short)
    5. **The Core Idea** section - Read these concept pages:
       - **Zero-Interaction Principle** (/core/zero-interaction-principle)
       - **System Architecture** (/core/system-architecture)
       - **Shared Biological State** (/core/shared-biological-state)
       - **Distributed Intelligence** (/core/distributed-intelligence)
    6. **Status and Roadmap** (/status/status-and-roadmap)

    This path explains what the system does, why it is designed this way, and where it stands today.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Deep dive: full understanding">
    For researchers, engineers, caregivers, or anyone evaluating LifeSphere in depth, work through the site in this order:

    1. **Start Here** section (all pages)
    2. **The Core Idea** section (all pages)
    3. **Design and Ethics** section (all pages)
    4. **Status** section (all pages, especially **Honest Limits** at /status/honest-limits)
    5. **Subsystems** - For each subsystem, read the overview first, then components, then design decisions:
       - Vital32 (/vital32/overview)
       - HollowRover (/hollowrover/overview)
       - SphereAI (/sphereai/overview)
       - EntryGuard (/entryguard/overview)
       - HollowCore (/hollowcore/overview)
    6. **Medication** (/medication/overview)
    7. **Intelligence** (/ai/overview)
    8. **Scenarios** (/scenarios/fall) - Walk through real-world response flows
    9. **Reference** (/reference/glossary) - Lookup tables and formulas
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How the site is organized

The documentation follows the structure of the system itself. The **Foundations** section covers purpose, architecture, and ethics. The **Subsystems** section explains each hardware and software component in detail. The **Intelligence** section describes the AI models and their design goals. The **Scenarios** section walks through concrete situations the system is designed to handle. The **Reference** section contains formulas, state fields, and a glossary.
